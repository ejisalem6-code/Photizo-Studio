Licensing

This page explains how templates and creator content may be used within Photizo Studio.

Using a Template

  • Saving or using a template you did not create is for reuse and substitution within your own project — it may not be extracted and re-published as a new standalone template.
  • Downloading, exporting, or editing with a permitted template triggers a payout to its original creator.

Submitting Your Own Content as a Template

  • You may opt any video you own into the Templates section, permitting other users to use it as a template.
  • You retain ownership of your original content; permitting template use does not transfer ownership to Photizo Studio or to other users.
  • Payout rate on your template's usage is tied to your subscription tier at the time of payout: 5% (Spark), 10% (Radiant), 15% (Lambent Diamond).

PCDL Hub Content

Sermons, teachings, and devotionals in PCDL Hub are uploaded solely by the App owner or approved Administrators on behalf of Ministers of the Gospel, and remain the property of their respective ministers/rights holders.
