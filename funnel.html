One app for creating, learning, and earning

Photizo comes from the Greek photizo, "to give light." Ministries and creators get professional tools in one place, and get paid when others use their work.

Editing suite

Photo and video editing with every standard feature, plus logo design. Work auto-saves to Photizo Space, built-in storage.

Editable templates

Start from ready-made designs. Creators can let others use their videos as templates and earn 5% to 15% each time.

Pleroma AI

Reviews your photos and videos, suggests what to add or remove, and recommends scriptures that match your theme. It's a reviewer, not a chatbot.

Sophia Library

Approved sermons, documentaries, and self-development videos. Content is uploaded only by the owner and approved Admins.

Safe community

Comment and chat with per-comment visibility, no follow feature, and no screenshots or screen recording of reels.

Coming soon

Create & Compose

Import footage, drag in audio, or start from a template. A full editing workspace is on its way, with a multi-track timeline for video and standalone audio.

Standalone audio editing

Open a sermon recording, song, or voice note by itself. Trim, fade, and mix it on its own track.

Video and audio together

Layer clips, music, and voice on one timeline with a live preview and an audio mixer.
