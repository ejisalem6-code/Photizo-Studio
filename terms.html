Terms and Conditions

These Terms and Conditions ("Terms") govern access to and use of the Photizo Studio application and website ("the App"), including all editing tools, templates, storage, the PCDL Hub, and related features. By creating an account, you agree to be bound by these Terms.

1. Accounts

  • Every user must create an individual account to use the App.
  • Sign-up is available via Email or KingsChat direct login, Email and Password, or Phone Number and Password (phone and password may be used for login only, not initial sign-up).
  • A user profile consists of a profile picture, display name, and the user's posted reels.

2. Subscription Tiers

  • Spark (Free): Ad-supported; limited Pleroma AI (2x/24h); no PCDL Hub access; 5% template creator payout.
  • Radiant (5 Espees / ₦10,250): PCDL Hub access; Pleroma AI (10x/24h); 10% template creator payout; glow/star badge.
  • Lambent Diamond (15 Espees / ₦30,750): PCDL Hub access; unlimited Pleroma AI; 15% template creator payout; diamond badge; fully ad-free, no exceptions.
  • Radiant and Lambent Diamond members receive a visible badge in place of a separate identity-verification process.
  • The App's recommendation algorithm favors paid-tier content for greater visibility.

Storage — "Photizo Space"

  • All user work auto-saves automatically, except where an item has been deleted.
  • Deleted items move to a Trash folder rather than being erased immediately. A double confirmation is required both to move an item to Trash and to permanently delete it.
  • The App does not auto-purge Trash — items remain until manually and permanently deleted.
  • Items in Trash continue to count toward storage usage.
  • "Unlimited" storage under paid tiers is subject to a soft fair-use cap of 600GB.

4. Templates and Creator Monetization

  • Users may permit their own videos to be used as templates by other users.
  • A creator payout is triggered when another user downloads, exports, or edits content using that creator's permitted template.
  • Payout rates are tied to the creator's own subscription tier: 5% (Spark), 10% (Radiant), 15% (Lambent Diamond).

5. PCDL Hub

  • PCDL Hub hosts messages from Ministers of the Gospel — sermons, teachings, and devotionals.
  • Access is restricted to Radiant and Lambent Diamond subscribers.
  • Content may only be uploaded by the App owner or approved Administrators — there is no general user upload or submission flow.

6. Reels, Comments, and Social Interaction

  • There is no "follow" feature — social interaction happens through chat and comments only.
  • Reels and templates display blurred by default and require an active network connection to view in full.
  • Once saved by another user, a reel's save cannot be revoked by the original poster; the poster is notified of who saved it.
  • Screenshots and screen recording of reels are not permitted.
  • Comment/chat visibility may be set to Public, Only Associates, or Only the poster, and can be changed at any time.
  • "Associate" status is created automatically after a comment-and-reply exchange between two users — no request/accept step is required.

7. Pleroma AI

  • Pleroma AI is a review feature — not a chatbot — that analyzes a photo or video and suggests edits, including scripture suggestions matching the content's theme.
  • Usage is limited by tier: Spark — 2x/24h, Radiant — 10x/24h, Lambent Diamond — unlimited.
  • Users choose which suggested changes, if any, to apply.

8. Administrators

  • There is no application, sign-up, or request process to become an Administrator.
  • The App owner ("Super Admin") is the only account able to grant Administrator access, exclusively via a direct, one-off invite.
  • Each Administrator invite is valid for 12 hours from issuance and is single-use — an unaccepted invite expires automatically and cannot be reused.
  • This process is identical on the website and within the App.

9. Changes to These Terms

Photizo Studio may update these Terms from time to time to reflect changes to the App's features, pricing, or policies. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Terms.

10. Contact

Questions regarding these Terms should be directed to the App owner or Administrators through the App's designated support channel.
